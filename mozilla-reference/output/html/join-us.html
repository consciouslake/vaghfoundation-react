Drive change with us

The Internet is built by people like you. Join us.

We’re not waiting for a better tech future. We’re building it—together.

At Mozilla Foundation, we believe the Internet should lift us all. It should be safe, open, and shaped by the people who use it. That means people like you.

By joining our community, you’re adding your voice to a movement that’s making good the default. You’ll help advance digital rights, support trustworthy technology, and stand for an internet where everyone can create, connect, and thrive.

You don’t need to be a coder to make change. You just need to care about the future—and get involved.

Add your voice and take a stand on how technology should impact your life. Make good the new default.
